    I am a multi-disciplinary creator who thrives at the intersection of software engineering, robotics, and the creative arts. As a Full-Stack Developer, I specialize in building scalable backend architectures and multi-platform applications using React, Python, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.

    My engineering journey extends beyond the screen into physical systems. As a Robotics Instructor with STEMbees and a mentor for the FIRST Global Challenge, I teach and build using Java, Python, Arduino, and 3D printing. This hands-on hardware experience deeply influences how I write software, teaching me to build robust, fault-tolerant systems.

    When I am not writing code or wiring circuits, I am immersed in music. I play the keyboard and my 5-string bass guitar, and I frequently look for ways to merge my love for music with technology by architecting audio-focused software applications. I am driven by a desire to innovate, educate, and build solutions that leave a lasting impact.
